What we can do for you

How a visit goes

Simple. On purpose.

1

Book, or just walk in

WhatsApp, call or turn up. All of them work.

2

Sit down with the nurse

She asks, listens and examines. Bring your clinic card and any medication you're taking.

3

Leave with a plan

Treatment on the spot where possible. A referral when it needs a doctor, a scan or a hospital.

4

We check in

For ongoing things like blood pressure or pregnancy, your next visit is booked before you go.

IV drips

Sit back. Rehydrate.

Four drips, one rule. The nurse checks you first: blood pressure, medical history, allergies. If a drip isn't right for you, she'll tell you.

For employers

Your people, looked after.

We do pre-employment medicals and routine medical surveillance at the clinic. We also come to you. Wellness days at your premises, with blood pressure and glucose checks done on site.
